# xGameFi — Pitch Deck Image Prompts

Image-generation prompts for the full-slide visual hooks in `pitch-deck-draft-xGameFi.pptx`.
Numbers match the `[FULL-SLIDE IMAGE — see image prompt #N]` placeholders in the deck.

## Prompt #1 — Slide 1 (Title / hero)

A full-bleed hero image for a game-commerce tech brand. A sleek obsidian-black background with faint horizontal scanlines and a subtle grid receding into depth. Centered negative space reserved for a wordmark, framed by clipped/beveled corner accents glowing in acid-lime (#C6F432-style neon). Off to the sides, ghosted holographic UI fragments — a storefront card, a QR code, a floating "PAID" badge — rendered as translucent cyan-green wireframes. Mood: confident, premium, cyberpunk-lite, "neon overdrive." Cinematic lighting, high contrast, dark tech-startup aesthetic. Leave the center and lower third clean and uncluttered for overlaid title text. 16:9.

## Prompt #2 — Slide 2 (Problem)

A conceptual split-composition illustration contrasting chaos vs. order, dark editorial tech style. LEFT side: several small indie game studios, each isolated in its own box, redundantly hand-building the same tangled plumbing — checkout forms, shopping carts, delivery pipes, fraud shields — drawn as messy, duplicated, overlapping pipes and wiring in muted grey with warning-orange stress accents; a sense of wasted, repeated effort. RIGHT side: a single clean, glowing acid-lime commerce layer — one unified rail — that all the studios could plug into, elegant and minimal. Obsidian background, faint scanlines, isometric diagram feel. Mood: frustration resolving into clarity. Keep the upper-center area readable for an overlaid headline and leave the lower third calmer for bullet text. 16:9.
