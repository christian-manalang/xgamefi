# xGameFi

Every game studio that wants to sell in-game items pays the same hidden tax: before it can take a single payment, it has to build its own checkout, storefront, fraud handling, payout system, and item-delivery pipeline — none of which is the game. xGameFi removes that tax. A studio connects its existing item catalogue and, within hours, gets a branded storefront where players pay with a Stellar wallet in one click — no password, no account. Every payment is verified before anything moves; the studio receives its net share instantly, the item is delivered to the player automatically, and a built-in peer-to-peer resale market, referrals, and promotions all come included. This is where xGameFi matters to Stellar: it isn't a wrapper that name-drops blockchain for optics — every purchase, studio payout, resale escrow, and referral reward is a genuine Stellar transaction, settled on-chain in seconds at a fraction of the cost and delay of card processors or 30%-cut platform stores. It is a repeatable, non-speculative, retail-scale use case — micro-transaction game commerce — that drives real consumer payment volume onto the network and expands Stellar's footprint in gaming beyond wallets and trading into everyday spending. Every studio onboarded compounds that volume.

**For reviewers:** xGameFi is feature-complete and runs end to end today — you can watch a real purchase settle on Stellar and an item land with the player live, in seconds, on a public transaction feed. The quickest way to grasp the product is that single flow: open a studio's storefront, connect a wallet, buy an item, and watch "payment in" become "item delivered" in real time. From here, the roadmap deepens Stellar integration exactly where it adds the most user value — anchor-based fiat on-ramps so a player can pay by card with no prior crypto, passkey smart-wallet sign-in that removes seed phrases entirely, support for established stablecoins, and on-chain item ownership so resale becomes fully trustless across games. Go-to-market starts in the Philippines, where mobile gaming and everyday e-wallet habits make wallet payments intuitive, then expands across APAC and globally through the Stellar ecosystem. As you evaluate, we invite you to weigh three things: the sharpness of the problem-solution fit for indie studios, the fact that settlement is genuinely on-chain rather than cosmetic, and an integration roadmap built on Stellar's own proven primitives. There is much more to explore in the full submission — thank you for reading.
